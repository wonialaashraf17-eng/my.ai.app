What is cryptocurrency?

Digital assets that use cryptography and often blockchain networks to record transactions. Different projects have different risks and purposes.

⌁

Understand volatility

Crypto prices can move sharply. Learn about risk, fees, scams, and diversification before making financial decisions.

⌕

Read market data

Price, trading volume, market capitalization, and percentage change each tell a different part of the story.

♢

Protect your account

Use unique passwords and two-factor authentication. Never share seed phrases or send funds to strangers promising guaranteed returns.
